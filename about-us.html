Our Story

Founded in 2018, Booktels Kenya emerged from a vision to revolutionize event planning in East Africa. Starting in a small office in Westlands, Nairobi, we recognized the need for professional event planning that honors Kenyan traditions while meeting international standards.

Today, we've grown into Kenya's most trusted event planning company, having successfully organized over 500 events across the country. From traditional Maasai weddings in the Rift Valley to corporate conferences in Nairobi's business district, our journey reflects our commitment to excellence and cultural authenticity.
